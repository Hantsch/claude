3 · Board the sprint as a mini kanban

The active sprint is drawn as four lanes — Ready, Build, Review, Done — with one card per story. A card shows its deliverables as dots, and the agent working on it sits on the card itself: role, model, elapsed time and the live tool call. Where a story stands and who is on it reads in one glance; what waits on you and what comes next follow as slim strips under the board.

Uses: Box flexDirection="row" lanes, borderStyle="single" borderColor per lane, backgroundColor, Box hover on cards — terminal folds the four lanes into a 2×2 grid.

Trade-off: the clearest picture of a running sprint and of who works on what, but it only shows the sprint — milestones beyond the current one shrink to a single “Next” strip, and a sprint with more than three stories in one lane makes that lane tall (in the terminal, where 54 cells allow only two lanes side by side, even more so).
