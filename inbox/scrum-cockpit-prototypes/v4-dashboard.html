4 · Dashboard metrics first

Numbers before lists: four KPI tiles on top, then the sprint's burndown against its ideal line, velocity over the last seven sprints and where the sprint's deliverables stand. Agents shrink to a dense table, and what waits on you stays amber. Reads like a team dashboard: good for "are we on track?", less so for "what do I press next?".

Uses: Svg (burndown, velocity, sparkline, VS Code only), Text size="title" tabular, Box borderStyle — the terminal draws the charts in eighth blocks and the tiles as boxes side by side.

Trade-off: the richest picture of progress, but it needs history ai-scrum does not record today — velocity and a daily burndown would have to be derived from the sprint files' dates or kept by the mod in $.store. In a narrow terminal pane the charts eat half the height, so the agents move further down than in any other variant.
