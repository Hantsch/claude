2 · Timeline one rail from the last milestone to the next

The roadmap becomes a vertical rail: done milestones in green, the current one glowing in Claude orange and expanded in place, future ones hollow. Under the current node the sprint branches into its stories, and every running agent hangs under the story it works on — who works on what is read off the shape, not off a list. What waits on you is a flag pinned to the current node.

Uses: Box borderStyle / borderColor for the rail and branches, Text color, backgroundColor for the agent chips, Text size="heading" — VS Code draws the rail as CSS lines, the terminal as ┃ ├─ └─ cells.

Trade-off: the strongest sense of "where are we" and the clearest link between agents and stories, but the tree is deep: with five or more stories and agents it grows long, and done milestones further back must be folded to keep the rail short.
