5 · Focus what matters now, nothing else at full volume

Three quiet blocks instead of seven sections: Now (what is running, one thin progress line, the live agents), Your move (the one thing only you can do, with the pane's only colored button) and Later (milestones, stories, roadmap and versions folded into one-liners that open under the pointer). No frames — size, weight and whitespace do the work, and the accent color is reserved for the action and the live dots.

Uses: Text size="title" / "heading" / "caption", weight, dimColor, Box display="none" hover={{ display: 'flex' }} (hover reveal), Button variant="primary", Button plain dimColor — no borders, no Svg needed.

Trade-off: the calmest and the fastest to read at a glance, but it decides for you what “your move” is — the ranking (waiting-on-you before an unplanned milestone before a loose story) has to be right, and the full story list is one hover away instead of always visible. In the terminal a hover only works with mouse support on; the keyboard fallback is a hotkey per line.
