1 · Mission Control cards, one per concern

Every section becomes its own framed card with a colored edge for its state: the current milestone as a hero with a progress ring, the sprint as a story table with deliverable bars, agents with their live activity, and anything that waits on you in amber. Closest to the current layout, just with structure and color.

Uses: Box borderStyle="round" borderColor, backgroundColor, Text size="heading", Svg (ring, VS Code only) — terminal draws the bar in eighth blocks.

Trade-off: the most familiar and the easiest to scan, but the borders cost two rows per card — in a short terminal the agents card is pushed below the fold once a sprint has more than six stories.
