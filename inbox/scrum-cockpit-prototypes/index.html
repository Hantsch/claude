Scrum cockpit · five directions

Each page shows the same project state — sprint S13 running, two agents at work, two things waiting on you — drawn twice: in the VS Code extension's pane and in the terminal's fullscreen pane, with the band above the prompt in both. Only elements and props the mod engine offers on that surface are used, so whichever you pick can be built as drawn.

1Mission Control

One framed card per concern: a hero for the milestone with a progress ring, the sprint as a story table, agents, a waiting-on-you card in amber.

Closest to today, just structured. Familiar, easy to scan.

2Timeline

A vertical rail of milestones; the current one opens into its sprint, its stories, and the agents hanging under the story they work on.

Best for "where are we and who does what".

3Board

A mini kanban of the running sprint — Ready, Build, Review, Done — with the working agent as a badge on its card.

Best while a sprint runs; thin between sprints.

4Dashboard

Metrics first: KPI tiles, a burndown, velocity per sprint, story status, then a dense agent table.

The "fanciest"; needs history ai-scrum does not record yet.

5Focus

Calm and typographic: what is happening now, the one thing that is your move, everything else folded into lines that open on hover.

Least noise; the rest is a hover away.
